常见问题

需要联网吗？
不需要。9000 多个汉字的笔顺数据都内置在 App 里，离线也能正常使用。

描红练习总是判定写错？
请按笔顺从起笔写到收笔，方向不要反；笔画大致落在灰色字形上即可，不需要写得完全一样。同一笔连续写错 3 次后会自动显示提示。

笔画名称好像不准确？
笔画名称是程序根据书写轨迹自动识别的，个别复杂笔画（例如横折弯钩、横撇弯钩）可能有偏差，笔顺动画本身不受影响。欢迎来信反馈。

怎么打印字帖？
点“生成字帖”，选择“范字字帖”或“空白练字纸”，调整版式后点“打印”，或点“导出 PDF”保存文件。

支持哪些语言？
App 界面支持简体中文、English、日本語和한국어，会跟随系统语言自动切换。

没有声音？
请检查设备的静音开关和音量，以及“设置 > 练习”中的音效开关是否开启。

FAQ

Do I need an internet connection?
No. Stroke data for more than 9,000 characters is built into the app, so it works fully offline.

Tracing practice keeps marking my strokes wrong.
Write each stroke in the correct direction, from its start to its end. It only needs to roughly follow the gray outline. After three wrong attempts on the same stroke, a hint appears.

A stroke name looks wrong.
Stroke names are detected automatically from the writing path, so a few complex strokes may be named incorrectly. The stroke-order animation itself is not affected. Feedback is welcome.

How do I print a worksheet?
Tap “Make worksheet”, choose “Model worksheet” or “Blank grid paper”, adjust the layout, then tap “Print” or “Export PDF”.

Which languages are supported?
The app is available in Simplified Chinese, English, Japanese and Korean, and follows your device language.

There’s no sound.
Check your device’s silent switch and volume, and make sure sound is turned on in Settings > Practice.
